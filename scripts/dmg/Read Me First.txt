BARDIC: local, private voice dictation for your Mac
====================================================

Hold a key, talk, let go, and polished text appears wherever your cursor is.
Everything runs on your Mac. No account, no cloud.

WHAT YOU NEED
  - A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later). Intel Macs can't run it.
  - macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
  - About 4 GB of free disk space for the speech and AI models.

INSTALLING
  1. Drag Bardic onto the Applications folder in this window.
  2. Open Bardic from Applications. macOS will say it couldn't verify that
     Bardic is free of malware. That's because Bardic is a free app that isn't
     registered with Apple. Click Done (not Move to Trash).
  3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
     "Open Anyway" next to the message about Bardic. Confirm with your password.
  4. A setup window walks you through the rest:
       - allow the Microphone
       - allow Accessibility (so the dictation key works in every app and
         Bardic can paste the text)
       - choose your dictation key
       - wait for the models to download (about 3 GB, once). After the
         download, Bardic spends up to 10 more minutes preparing the speech
         model for your Mac. That happens the first time and again after a
         macOS update. Other launches take seconds and work offline.

Bardic lives in the menu bar (the waveform icon at the top right), not the Dock.

USING IT
  - Hold Right Option, speak, release.
  - Double-tap the key for hands-free; tap once more to finish.
  - Esc cancels.

UPDATES
  When a new version is out, the Bardic menu shows "Bardic x.y.z is available".
  Download it, drag it into Applications to replace the old one, and repeat
  step 3 above. macOS treats each version as a new app, so allow it again:
  in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility, select Bardic,
  click the minus (-) button, then open Bardic and follow its prompt.

PROBLEMS?
  Open Bardic's menu > Settings > About, click "Copy debug info", and paste it
  into a message or a GitHub issue: https://github.com/bardic-app/bardic/issues
  It contains no dictated text.
